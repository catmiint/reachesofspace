Referral Program

If you refer new members, you and the members can earn rewards!
Joining via referral (mentioned upon joining in the world join message or via the intro on discord) gives the new member and the referrer 2 vials of stardust!
Additionally, there are additional bonuses per referral for a particular referrer, listed below!
  • 5 referrals - 1 Rare MYO slot
  • 10 referrals - 1 Blue Moon Jawbreaker
Additional rewards may come in the future as we grow!
