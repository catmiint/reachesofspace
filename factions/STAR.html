STAR

The Stellar Trade Aggregate Realm, or STAR as it’s come to be known, is an organization that regulates intergalactic trade independent from any planet’s or solar system’s government. To avoid showing favor to any one government, STAR has a space ship called the Starship Sideris that moves from solar system to solar system.

The SS Sideris is a luxury starship that is more like a moving commercial metropolis than a transport vessel. Getting to sell goods or services on the SS Sideris is an ordeal in and of itself due to all of the forms and licenses involved because if there’s one thing that STAR loves—it’s regulations.

Regulators

STAR creates rules and regulations around all aspects of trade and commerce across the galaxy. Some of these regulations are quite reasonable and meant to protect consumers and governments from the horrors of capitalism. Other regulations are the horrors of capitalism. Stepping up to enforce STAR’s rules are its Regulators. These Regulators are powerful people and hold the intergalactic economy in their claws. Gummisaurs that find themselves as STAR Regulators are known less as creatures of wanderlust and more as traveling robber-barons.
